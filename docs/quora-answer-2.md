# Quora 回答 2

## 问题
What's a better alternative to MBTI?

## 回答内容（直接复制粘贴）

I've tested a lot of personality systems — MBTI, Enneagram, Big Five, CliftonStrengths — and the one that actually surprised me was the **I Ching birth hexagram system**.

### Why MBTI falls short

MBTI only has 16 types, which sounds reasonable until you realize that roughly half the population tests as one of four types (INFJ, INFP, ENFP, INTJ). The system is too coarse to actually be useful for self-understanding. It also has well-documented test-retest reliability issues — about 50% of people get a different type when they retake it 5 weeks later.

### The I Ching approach

The I Ching system works differently. Instead of answering questionnaires, you calculate your birth hexagram based on your birth date and time. This gives you one of 64 hexagrams, each of which describes:
- Your core strengths and blind spots
- Your natural leadership/communication style
- How you handle stress
- What kind of partners/teams you work best with

That's 64 types vs MBTI's 16 — four times the resolution.

### The compatibility angle

The thing that really blew my mind was the compatibility matrix. Instead of "compatible / incompatible," the I Ching system maps out all 4096 possible pairings between the 64 hexagrams, with specific descriptions of how that dynamic plays out. It's not generic "opposites attract" advice — it's specific to how your particular archetype interacts with theirs.

### My take

I'm not saying the I Ching is "magic." What I am saying is that after 3,000 years of refinement, it's an incredibly dense and specific system for describing human patterns. It feels like the difference between a 16-color paint set and a 64-color set — suddenly you can see shades you didn't even know existed.

If you've outgrown MBTI and want something with more depth, it's worth spending an hour calculating your birth hexagram and reading through the description.
