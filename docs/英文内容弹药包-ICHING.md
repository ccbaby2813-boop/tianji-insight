## 四、Quora 前 5 答（长尾 SEO，前 10 答不放外链）

**Q1: Is the I Ching accurate?**
> "Accurate" depends on what you ask it to do. If you want it to predict a specific outcome, the I Ching is not a scientific predictor — and the honest answer is that no system is. But if you use it as a personality/decision framework (which is how it's used in the Chinese tradition beyond fortune-telling), it's remarkably coherent: your birth date computes to one of 64 hexagrams, each describing a distinct human archetype — strengths, blind spots, tendencies. Think of it less as prophecy and more as a 3,000-year-old personality classification system. It's deterministic, not random.

**Q2: MBTI vs I Ching — which is better for understanding personality?**
> They answer different questions. MBTI is self-reported preferences across 4 dichotomies → 16 types. The I Ching computes your hexagram from birth date/time → 64 archetypes, with 4,096 pair interactions for compatibility. MBTI is easier to grasp and widely validated; the I Ching is older, more granular, and doesn't rely on a questionnaire (so it can't be gamed by how you answer). Neither is "better" — they're complementary lenses. If MBTI felt too coarse for you, the 64-type system is worth a look.

**Q3: How do I find my birth hexagram?**
> Your birth hexagram (本命卦) is computed using Plum Blossom Numerology: your year, month, day, and hour are converted through the 8 trigrams to derive a hexagram. There's a classical calculation method you can do by hand, but it's tedious — most people use an app or tool that does the math. I built one (I Ching Insight) that also adds an AI interpretation across 12 personality dimensions. For the traditional route, search "Plum Blossom Numerology birth hexagram calculation" — there are step-by-step guides.

**Q4: Is the I Ching related to astrology?**
> Only loosely. Western astrology maps the planets' positions at your birth; the I Ching is based on numerical computation from your birth date/time (yin-yang line transformations), rooted in Chinese metaphysics alongside the Five Elements and Plum Blossom Numerology. Both give you a framework for self-reflection, but the mechanisms are entirely different — one is astronomical, one is arithmetic-symbolic.

**Q5: What does each I Ching hexagram mean for personality?**
> Each of the 64 hexagrams is a stack of 6 yin/yang lines — a compound archetype. For example: Hexagram 1 (☰ The Creative) = natural leader, visionary, driven but can be hard on self; Hexagram 2 (☷ The Receptive) = grounded, nurturing, wise; Hexagram 29 (☵ The Abyss) = deep-feeling, resilient, often misunderstood. A full interpretation looks at strengths, blind spots, emotional style, career energy, and relationship patterns — which is what modern tools like I Ching Insight do automatically.
