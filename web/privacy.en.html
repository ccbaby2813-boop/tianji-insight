I Ching Insight Privacy Policy

What is this about? I Ching Insight Privacy Policy, explaining how we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Key takeaways: This article covers the core concepts, practical applications, and common questions about I Ching Insight privacy policy.

Updated: August 2026

Introduction

Beijing Wuyuan Digital Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter "we") deeply understands the importance of personal information to you. We will take corresponding security protection measures in accordance with legal requirements to protect your personal information safely and controllably.

This policy applies to all services provided by I Ching Insight, including WeChat Mini Program, web version, Android/iOS applications.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information: When you register a I Ching Insight account, we will collect your WeChat openid or email address to create and identify your account.

1.2 Personal Information: When you create a profile, we will collect your basic information such as nickname, gender, and date of birth to generate I Ching personality interpretations.

1.3 Usage Data: We will automatically collect device information, operation logs, insight records, etc. when you use the services to optimize your service experience.

1.4 Location Information: We will not actively collect your location information unless you explicitly authorize it.

2. Use of Information

2.1 The information we collect will be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing core services such as I Ching personality interpretation, deep insight, and soul matching
  • Optimizing product features and user experience
  • Sending service notifications (such as matching results, letter replies, etc.)
  • Conducting data analysis and research to improve service quality
  • Complying with legal requirements

2.2 We will not use your personal information for other purposes without your consent.

3. Storage of Information

3.1 Your personal information will be stored on servers within China.

3.2 We adopt industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including but not limited to encrypted transmission, access control, and regular backups.

3.3 We will retain your personal information only for the time necessary to achieve the purpose. After the retention period, it will be deleted or anonymized.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

4.1 We will not share your personal information with third parties unless:

  • Obtaining your explicit consent
  • According to legal requirements or government agency requirements
  • Sharing with our affiliates (limited to what is necessary to provide services)
  • Involved in merger, acquisition or asset transfer

4.2 In the soul matching feature, we will display necessary information such as your nickname and hexagram to the matching object, but will not disclose your true identity.

5. Your Rights

5.1 Access and Correction: You can access and correct your personal information at any time.

5.2 Deletion: You can apply to delete your personal information, except where retention is required by laws and regulations.

5.3 Account Cancellation: You can apply to cancel your account. After cancellation, we will delete your personal information.

5.4 Withdrawal of Consent: You can withdraw your consent to our collection and use of your personal information, but this may affect the use of some services.

6. Protection of Minors

6.1 We attach great importance to the protection of personal information of minors.

6.2 If you are a minor, please read this policy accompanied by your guardian and use our services with your guardian's consent.

6.3 This product is only for users aged 18 and above. The date picker restricts selecting birth dates for users under 18.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

7.1 We may use cookies or similar technologies to collect and use your information to provide a better service experience.

7.2 You can manage or delete cookies through browser settings, but this may affect the use of some features.

8. Updates to Privacy Policy

8.1 We may revise this policy from time to time, and the revised content will be published on the I Ching Insight platform.

8.2 If the revision involves significant changes, we will notify you in a prominent way.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, opinions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, or need to exercise your rights, please contact us through the following ways:

  • Contact Email: ccbaby2813@139.com
