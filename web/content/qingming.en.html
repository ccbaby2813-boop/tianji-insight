Pure Brightness Wellness

What is this about? Pure Brightness (Apr 4-6) complete wellness guide: Wood element, Liver organ. Three phenological phases: The paulownia tree begins to flower, Field mice turn into quails, Rainbows begin to appear. Qingming, the sky is clear and the earth is bright; clear the liver and brighten the eyes. Avoid staying in damp places for long; go for walks in nature, climb high and look far, drink chrysanthemum tea, and keep your mind at ease. Eat Qingtuan (green rice ball), Indian aster (Malantou). Avoid Excessive grief.

Key takeaways: This article covers the core concepts, practical applications, and common questions about pure brightness wellness.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The sky is clear and the earth is bright; keep your will calm and peaceful, and gather your spirit qi."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Regulating the Spirit with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: During Qingming, the sky is clear and the earth is bright; one should keep the mind calm and peaceful, gather the spirit, and not let it scatter outward.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'Gather your spirit qi.' During Qingming ancestor worship, emotions can easily fluctuate; steady your mind and don't let excessive grief damage your liver qi.

Action Items

  • Drink more chrysanthemum tea to clear the liver and brighten the eyes
  • Climb high and look far to soothe and disperse liver qi
  • Don't let sadness harm the liver excessively

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pure Brightness?

Pure Brightness is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around Apr 4-6. At this time: The paulownia tree begins to flower, Field mice turn into quails, Rainbows begin to appear. It marks the depth of spring.

What is the wellness focus for Pure Brightness?

The wellness focus for Pure Brightness is Clear the liver, Brighten the eyes, Dispel dampness. Qingming, the sky is clear and the earth is bright; clear the liver and brighten the eyes. Avoid staying in damp places for long; go for walks in nature, climb high and look far, drink spinach tea, and keep your mind at ease.

What should I eat during Pure Brightness?

During Pure Brightness, eat Qingtuan (green rice ball), spinach (Malantou), Wolfberry sprouts, Chrysanthemum, Green tea. Recommended recipes: spinach tossed with dried tofu, Wolfberry sprout and pork liver soup, Green tea and spinach drink.

What should I avoid during Pure Brightness?

During Pure Brightness, avoid: Excessive grief, Rich, fatty, and sweet foods, Staying long in damp places.
