Grain Buds Wellness

What is this about? Grain Buds (May 20-22) complete wellness guide: Fire element, Heart organ. Three phenological phases: Bitter herbs flourish, Frail grasses wither, Wheat harvest arrives. During Grain Buds, damp-heat rises. Clear heat and resolve dampness. Avoid excessive cold and greasy food; eat more bitter herbs, winter melon, and sour plum soup. Exercise in the early morning; keep an open mind and don't be impatient. Eat Bitter herbs, Cucumber. Avoid Fatty, sweet, and greasy foods.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"Let the spirit know no anger, let the flower of life bloom, let the breath flow outward, as if what you love lies beyond you."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Regulating the Spirit with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: Let the mind not be angered; let the blossoming beauty come to fruition; let the qi mechanism be free to disperse, as though one's beloved is outside.
Plain Language: The ancients said 'let the qi be released'—meaning let the body's qi flow freely. Don't suppress it or overdo coldness; let the sweat that needs to come out come out.

Action Items

  • Eat more bitter herbs and winter melon to clear heat and resolve dampness
  • Exercise in the early morning to keep the qi flowing and dispersing
  • Avoid excessive cold and greasy food to protect the spleen and stomach

Recommended Recipes

Cold Bitter Herb Salad

Ingredients: Bitter herbs · garlic · sesame oil

Steps: Blanch the bitter herbs, then toss with minced garlic and sesame oil

Effect: Clears heat and toxins, resolves dampness

Winter Melon and Pork Rib Soup

Ingredients: Winter melon · pork ribs · ginger

Steps: Cut the winter melon into chunks and stew with the pork ribs

Effect: Clears heat and dampness, promotes urination

Sour Plum Soup

Ingredients: Smoked plums · hawthorn · rock sugar

Steps: Boil the smoked plums and hawthorn for 30 minutes, then add rock sugar

Effect: Promotes body fluids, quenches thirst, and stimulates appetite

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Grain Buds?

Grain Buds is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around May 20-22. At this time: Bitter herbs flourish, Frail grasses wither, Wheat harvest arrives. It marks the depth of summer.

What is the wellness focus for Grain Buds?

The wellness focus for Grain Buds is Clear heat, Resolve dampness, Nourish yin. During Grain Buds, damp-heat rises. Clear heat and resolve dampness. Avoid excessive cold and greasy food; eat more bitter herbs, Chinese yam, and sour plum soup. Exercise in the early morning; keep an open mind and don't be impatient.

What should I eat during Grain Buds?

During Grain Buds, eat Bitter herbs, Cucumber, Winter melon, Mung bean soup, Sour plum soup. Recommended recipes: Cold Bitter Herb Salad, Winter Melon and Pork Rib Soup, Sour Plum Soup.

What should I avoid during Grain Buds?

During Grain Buds, avoid: Fatty, sweet, and greasy foods, Emotional agitation, Overindulgence in cold.
