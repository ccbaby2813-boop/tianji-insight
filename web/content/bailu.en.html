White Dew Wellness

What is this about? White Dew (Sep 7-9) complete wellness guide: Metal element, Lungs organ. Three phenological phases: Wild geese arrive, Swallows return south, Birds store provisions. As White Dew brings cooler weather, dryness gradually increases; moisten the lungs and nourish yin. Add clothing in the morning and evening to avoid catching a chill; eat more pears, almonds, and Chinese yam; hike to high places and breathe deeply to nourish the lungs. Eat Pear, Lily bulb. Avoid Cold and cooling foods that harm the lungs.

Key takeaways: This article covers the core concepts, practical applications, and common questions about white dew wellness.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The lungs are the root of qi, the dwelling of the corporeal soul (po). Their brilliance manifests in the body hair, and their fullness is in the skin. They are the greater yin within yang, and they communicate with the qi of autumn."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Six Cyclic Nodes and Visceral Manifestations
Interpretation: The lungs are the root of qi, house the corporeal soul, and their luster is in the skin and body hair. As White Dew brings cooler weather and dryness intensifies, one should moisten the lungs and nourish yin.
Plain Language: The ancients said autumn qi communicates with the lungs, and the lungs fear cold. During White Dew, mornings and evenings are cool; no more going shirtless. Protect the lungs from catching a chill, and counteract the dryness with moistening.

Action Items

  • Add clothing in the morning and evening to prevent cold pathogens from harming the lungs
  • Eat more pears and almonds to moisten the lungs and prevent dryness
  • Hike to high places and breathe deeply to strengthen lung qi

Recommended Recipes

Almond and Lily Congee

Ingredients: Almond · Lily bulb · Rice

Steps: Soak the almonds and lily bulb, then simmer with rice to make porridge

Effect: Moistens the lungs, relieves cough, and nourishes yin

Chinese Yam and Lotus Root Soup

Ingredients: Chinese yam · Lotus root · Pork ribs

Steps: Cut Chinese yam and lotus root into chunks and stew with pork ribs

Effect: Strengthens the spleen, moistens the lungs, and promotes fluid production

Stewed Snow Pear with Rock Sugar

Ingredients: Snow pear · Rock sugar · Goji berries

Steps: Core the snow pear, add rock sugar, and stew for 30 minutes

Effect: Moistens the lungs, generates fluids, and prevents dryness

Frequently Asked Questions

What is White Dew?

White Dew is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around Sep 7-9. At this time: Wild geese arrive, Swallows return south, Birds store provisions. It marks the depth of autumn.

What is the wellness focus for White Dew?

The wellness focus for White Dew is Moisten the lungs, Guard against dryness, Nourish yin. As White Dew brings cooler weather, dryness gradually increases; moisten the lungs and nourish yin. Add clothing in the morning and evening to avoid catching a chill; eat more pears, almonds, and Chinese yam; hike to high places and breathe deeply to nourish the lungs.

What should I eat during White Dew?

During White Dew, eat Pear, Lily bulb, Almond, white radish, Lotus root. Recommended recipes: Almond and Lily Congee, Chinese Yam and Lotus Root Soup, Stewed Snow Pear with Rock Sugar.

What should I avoid during White Dew?

During White Dew, avoid: Cold and cooling foods that harm the lungs, Pungent and irritating foods, Excessive sadness.
