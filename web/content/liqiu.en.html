Beginning of Autumn Wellness

What is this about? Beginning of Autumn (Aug 7-9) complete wellness guide: Metal element, Lungs organ. Three phenological phases: Cool winds arrive, White dew falls, Cold cicadas sing. Autumn dryness is gradually rising, so moistening the lungs comes first. Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The three months of autumn are called 'accommodation and equilibrium'; the heavenly qi is rushing, the earthly qi is clear. Sleep early and rise early, keeping the same schedule as the rooster."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Regulating the Spirit with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: As autumn begins, all things withdraw and constrict. One should sleep early and rise early, getting up with the rooster, and keep the will calm and peaceful.
Plain Language: The ancients said 'rise with the chickens' — meaning to follow the rooster's schedule: sleep early and rise early, don't stay up late, and let the body gradually pull back from its summer state of excitement.

Action Items

  • Try to fall asleep before 11 PM and wake with the dawn
  • Drink fewer cold beverages during the day; sip more warm water to soothe the throat
  • When feeling restless or irritable, take three deep breaths and draw your spirit back inward

Recommended Recipes

Tremella, Lily, and Snow Pear Stew

Ingredients: Tremella · Lily bulb · Snow pear · Rock sugar

Steps: Soak the tremella until rehydrated, tear into small florets; combine with lily bulbs and pear chunks, simmer for 40 minutes, then add rock sugar.

Effect: Moistens the lungs and generates fluids, relieving dry autumn cough.

Lotus Root and Pork Rib Soup

Ingredients: Lotus root · Pork ribs · Ginger

Steps: Cut the lotus root into chunks, stew with ribs for 1 hour, add salt just before serving.

Effect: Strengthens the spleen and nourishes yin, countering autumn fatigue.

Honey Pomelo Tea

Ingredients: Pomelo peel · Honey · Warm water

Steps: Slice the pomelo peel into thin slivers and steep; stir honey into warm water.

Effect: Moistens dryness, calms the spirit, and aids sleep.

Stay up less, avoid spicy foods, eat more pomelos and white fungus, sleep early and rise early, and draw your heart-mind back inward.
