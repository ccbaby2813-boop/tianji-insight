Judgment & Image

Judgment (卦辞)
Decrease: with sincerity, supreme good fortune, no blame. Perseverance is favorable. It is advantageous to have somewhere to go. What is the use? Two simple bowls suffice for the offering.
Image (象辞)
There is a lake below the mountain: Decrease. The superior person curbs anger and restrains desire.

Personality Deep Dive

You understand a truth: the more you possess, the more you are possessed. The Decrease hexagram has Gen (Mountain) above and Dui (Lake) below — the mountain is high and the lake is deep, the mountain pressing on the lake. This is the image of 'decreasing below to benefit above,' but the Judgment says 'Even two bamboo bowls can be offered in sacrifice.' Two simple ritual vessels are enough, showing that true sincerity lies not in form but in the heart. The Image says 'The superior person curbs anger and restrains desire.' Your core pattern is 'subtraction' — subtracting excess desire, excess emotion, excess relationships. It's not that you lack money; you simply think simple is better. The cost is that you can let go of anything, but you cannot let go of the few people you care about.

Emotional & Relationship Patterns

In love, you are a 'minimalist.' The lower trigram, Dui (Lake), represents inner joy; the upper trigram, Gen (Mountain), represents outer stillness — you are actually soft and deeply emotional inside, but externally you come across as restrained and detached. In the Five Elements, Earth generates Metal (Dui belongs to Metal). Your joy, filtered through your restraint, becomes a kind of 'quiet deep affection.' You don't love with grand gestures, but you quietly give when it matters most. What you need is a partner who 'understands your white space' — not someone talkative, but someone who can read the deep feeling behind your silence.

Strengths & Growth Blind Spots

Innate Talent
Your talent is 'decluttering' — the Image says 'the superior person curbs anger and restrains desire.' You have the ability to strip away the excess and return to the essential. In this age of information overload and material excess, your minimalism is a rare resource. You are suited for work that requires a 'less is more' mindset.
Growth Blind Spot
Your blind spot is 'over-restraint' — the Judgment speaks of 'sincerity,' calling you to be authentic and trustworthy. Sometimes you focus so much on 'subtracting' that you cut away things that shouldn't be cut — like emotion, like expression. Another blind spot is 'unable to let go of people' — you can throw away possessions, but you can't let go of your attachment to certain people.

Frequently Asked Questions about Hexagram 41 Sun

Quick answers about Sun personality, career, compatibility, and fortune

What is the personality of someone with Hexagram Sun?

Hexagram Sun represents the discerning one making wise trade-offs. People with this hexagram typically have distinctive traits and behavioral patterns, showing a unique style in relationships and career development. Understanding your hexagram personality helps you leverage strengths and address blind spots.

What careers suit Hexagram Sun?

People with Hexagram Sun often excel in financial planning, management, consulting, strategy. Choosing a career that matches your hexagram traits increases the likelihood of fulfillment and sustained growth. Of course, career choices should also consider personal interests, abilities, and market opportunities.

Which hexagram is most compatible with Sun?

Hexagram Sun pairs best with Yi (decrease then increase). The two hexagrams complement or resonate with each other in personality, making it easier to understand one another and build rapport in relationships and partnerships. Compatibility is a reference point; real relationships require mutual effort and commitment.

Is Hexagram Sun auspicious or inauspicious for fortune-telling?

Hexagram Sun: neutral: reducing below to benefit above, exercise moderation. In the I Ching, auspiciousness is not absolute; it indicates the attitude and action appropriate for a given situation. Even an inauspicious hexagram can be navigated safely by upholding correctness and acting cautiously. Even an auspicious hexagram can lead to misfortune if one becomes complacent.
