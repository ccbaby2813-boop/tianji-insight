Plum Blossom Numerology: The Ancient "Real-Time AI" for Everyday Decisions

Many people think the I Ching requires your exact birth chart and a master to cast a hexagram. In the Song Dynasty, Shao Yong offered a much lighter approach — no birth data needed, just grab a number or look at the clock.

Plum Blossom Numerology (梅花易数) is famous for its low barrier to entry — and its logic is strikingly similar to modern computing: casting is encoding, interpretation is decoding.

Why Plum Blossom is "real-time"

The method encodes "the current state of the world" into a pair of numbers, then maps them onto the 64 hexagrams. Three ways to cast:

  • Time casting: Convert the current year, month, day and hour into numbers. Divide by 8 to get the upper trigram, by 8 again for the lower trigram, by 6 for the moving line.
  • Number casting: Ask for two random numbers, or use anything you see — a license plate, a page number, a door number.
  • Omen casting: Even the first thing you see can become a hexagram — a flying bird, a vessel, a direction.

The logic in three sentences

  1. Upper trigram: first number ÷ 8, remainder maps to the eight trigrams (Qian 1, Dui 2, Li 3, Zhen 4, Xun 5, Kan 6, Gen 7, Kun 8).
  2. Lower trigram: second number ÷ 8, same remainder logic.
  3. Moving line: (a + b) ÷ 6, remainder picks the changing line. Original hexagram + changing hexagram + nuclear hexagram combine into the full reading.

If you can do elementary arithmetic, you can cast in under a minute. That is why it survived a thousand years: no birth data, available anywhere, anytime.

What it has to do with AI personality frameworks

Plum Blossom reveals a simple model: a person's current state can be digitized and mapped onto a structured symbolic system. That is exactly what AI personality products do today — feed in behavior, preferences and relationships, and map them onto a structured model. The I Ching's 64 hexagrams are one such model:

  • 64 hexagrams = 64 situational and personality archetypes
  • The moving line = the key turning point right now
  • The changing hexagram = the trend of development
