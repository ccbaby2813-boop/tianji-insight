Minor Snow Wellness

What is this about? Minor Snow (Nov 22-23) complete wellness guide: Water element, Kidneys organ. Three phenological phases: Rainbows hide and disappear, Sky energy rises, earth energy sinks, All closes and seals, winter sets in. Minor Snow brings cold weather; focus on tonifying the kidneys and nurturing inner storage. Dress warmly to avoid cold, don't sweat excessively, eat lamb and black beans, sleep early and rise late, and guard your spirit within. Eat Lamb, Beef. Avoid Catching cold from chill.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"Withdraw from cold and approach warmth; do not let the skin open and sweat, so that the qi is not repeatedly robbed. This is the response to winter qi and the way of nourishing storage."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · On Regulating the Spirit in Accordance with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: Move away from cold and toward warmth; avoid letting the skin open and sweat, which would rob the yang qi. This is the response to winter qi and the way of nurturing storage.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'Leave cold and seek warmth, do not let the skin open and sweat.' When Minor Snow turns cold, stay warm, avoid heavy sweating, and keep your yang qi tucked inside your body.
