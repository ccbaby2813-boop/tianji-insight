Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The spleen is related to Earth and rules the center. It constantly nourishes the four viscera in accordance with the four seasons, each season entrusting it with eighteen days."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Treatise on Taiyin and Yangming
Interpretation: Major Cold is the time at winter's end when Earth qi flourishes, and the Spleen-Earth takes command. As cold reaches its peak and spring is about to arrive, one should strengthen the spleen with warming nourishment to store energy for spring's growth and renewal.
Plain Language: The ancients said that when Spleen-Earth is in command, Major Cold is the last stretch of winter tonification. Strengthen the spleen with warming nourishing foods, stock up energy, and welcome the sprouting of spring.

Action Items

  • Eat more Chinese yam and longan to strengthen the spleen with warm nourishment
  • Massage the abdomen after meals to nurture the spleen and aid digestion
  • Soak your feet before bed to warm the body and welcome spring qi
