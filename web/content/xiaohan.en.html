Minor Cold Wellness

What is this about? Minor Cold (Jan 5-7) complete wellness guide: Water element, Kidneys organ. Three phenological phases: Wild geese begin flying north, Magpies start to build nests, Pheasants begin to crow. Minor Cold is the coldest time—warm the kidneys and consolidate essence. Dress warmly to avoid the chill, eat more lamb and walnuts, be less fearful and anxious, and calm your spirit to nurture your vitality. Eat Lamb, Beef. Avoid Exposure to cold injures the kidneys.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The kidney governs water, stores essence, and rules the bones. Fear injures the kidney; deep thought overcomes fear."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Great Treatise on the Correspondences of Yin and Yang
Interpretation: The kidney governs water, stores essence, and rules the bones. As the cold of Minor Cold deepens, we should warm the kidneys and consolidate essence, guarding against fear that can harm the kidney.
Plain Language: The ancients taught that 'fear injures the kidneys.' During Minor Cold, with cold weather and the New Year near, don't let tension and anxiety take over. When the mind is steady, kidney qi is steady—and keep warm.

Action Items

  • Dress warmly to protect the kidneys from wind and cold
  • Eat more lamb and walnuts to warm kidney yang
  • Calm the spirit and let go of fear and worry

Lamb and Chinese Yam Soup

Steps: Blanch the lamb, then simmer with Chinese yam for 1 hour

Black Rice and Red Date Porridge

Ingredients: Black rice · Red dates · Rice

Steps: Soak black rice, then cook into porridge with red dates and rice

Effect: Tonifies the kidneys, nourishes blood, and warms yang

Longan and Egg Soup

Ingredients: Longan · Eggs · Brown sugar

Effect: Nourishes blood, calms the spirit, and warms the kidneys

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Minor Cold?

Minor Cold is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around Jan 5-7. At this time: Wild geese begin flying north, Magpies start to build nests, Pheasants begin to crow. It marks the depth of winter.

What is the wellness focus for Minor Cold?

The wellness focus for Minor Cold is Warm the kidneys, Consolidate essence, Nourish the inner store. Minor Cold is the coldest time—warm the kidneys and consolidate essence. Dress warmly to avoid the chill, eat more longan and longans, be less fearful and anxious, and calm your spirit to nurture your vitality.

What should I eat during Minor Cold?

During Minor Cold, eat Lamb, Beef, Walnuts, Black rice, Red dates (jujube). Recommended recipes: Lamb and Chinese Yam Soup, Black Rice and Red Date Porridge, Longan and Egg Soup.

What should I avoid during Minor Cold?

During Minor Cold, avoid: Exposure to cold injures the kidneys, Excessive fear and anxiety, Staying up late depletes essence.
