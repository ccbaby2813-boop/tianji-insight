I Ching Personality Test: Find Your Birth Hexagram

Your birth date becomes one of 64 hexagram archetypes, then a full personality profile you can actually use.

I Ching Insight is an I Ching personality test that turns your birth date into one of the 64 hexagrams and a full personality profile. It is personality-first: the hexagrams are treated as a personality system, not as a coin-casting oracle.

What you get

I Ching Insight takes the 3,000-year-old Book of Changes and uses it the way you would use any modern personality framework. Instead of asking you a questionnaire, it calculates your birth hexagram from your date of birth and then reads it as an archetype: strengths, blind spots, values, communication style, and how you behave under pressure.

The result is not a vague mood card. Each of the 64 hexagrams has its own long-form profile, practical Do's and Don'ts, and a compatibility map against every other hexagram. Because 64 x 64 = 4,096, the full match matrix is something software handles far better than a book.

How it works: three steps

  1. Enter your birth date. Only the date is required — there is no questionnaire. If you know your birth hour, you can add the two-hour period for a more precise reading.
  2. Get your birth hexagram. The date is converted to the lunar calendar and reduced with the classical Plum Blossom Numerology method into an upper and a lower trigram, which combine into one of the 64 hexagrams, with a full reading.
  3. Compare with anyone. Place any two birth hexagrams side by side to explore all 4,096 combinations: attraction, friction patterns, and long-term potential.

The calculation is deterministic. The same date always produces the same hexagram, so you can check the logic yourself instead of trusting a black box.

Frequently asked questions

What is a birth hexagram?

A birth hexagram is the one hexagram out of the 64 in the I Ching that is tied to your date of birth. Each hexagram is six lines, either solid (yang) or broken (yin), and in I Ching Insight it is read as a personality archetype: your natural strengths, blind spots, values and communication style.

How is a birth hexagram calculated?

Your birth date is first converted to the lunar calendar, then reduced with the classical Plum Blossom Numerology method: the lunar year, month and day form the upper trigram, and the lower trigram is derived the same way, adding the two-hour birth period when you provide one. The two trigrams combine into one of the 64 hexagrams. The method is deterministic — the same input always returns the same hexagram, with no coin casting and no randomness.

Is this fortune telling?

No. I Ching Insight is a personality and self-reflection tool, not a prediction service. It uses the hexagram as a structured mirror for your patterns and decisions, and readings are offered for entertainment and educational purposes. You always make your own decisions.

How is this different from MBTI?

MBTI sorts people into 16 types using self-reported questionnaire answers; I Ching Insight derives one of 64 hexagram archetypes from your birth date, with no questionnaire to game. It also pairs any two hexagrams across all 64 x 64 combinations, so compatibility is a first-class part of the system rather than an add-on.

Is I Ching Insight free?

It is free to download and free to start: a short profile setup (nickname, birth date, gender) is all it takes to get your birth hexagram, and there is no signup form to fill in. An optional VIP membership, billed monthly or yearly through Google Play, adds more daily readings and unlocks the full deep analyses.

Do I need my birth time?

No — it is optional. When you know it, you can choose one of the twelve two-hour birth periods; if you leave it unset, the app uses 11:00-13:00 as the default. Only your birth date is required.
