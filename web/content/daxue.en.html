Major Snow Wellness

What is this about? Major Snow (Dec 6-8) complete wellness guide: Water element, Kidneys organ. Three phenological phases: The han-dan bird falls silent, Tigers begin to mate, The liting grass sprouts. Major Snow marks deep winter — consolidate the kidneys and store essence. Don't stay up late, don't catch cold. Eat more lamb, walnuts, and black sesame. Sleep early and rise late; calm the spirit and nourish the essence. Eat Lamb soup, Walnuts. Avoid Cold injures the kidneys.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The kidney governs hibernation; it is the root of closure and storage, and the residence of essence."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · On the Six Divisions and Visceral Manifestations
Interpretation: The kidney governs hibernation, is the root of sealing and storing, and the place where essence resides. During Major Snow, one should consolidate the kidney and store essence, making nurturing the storage the foundation.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'The kidney governs hibernation, the root of sealing and storage.' The kidney is the body's 'battery.' During Major Snow, consume less and store more — don't stay up late and drain your vital essence.

Action Items

  • Sleep early to nourish essence and avoid staying up late
  • Eat more lamb and walnuts to consolidate kidney essence
  • Exercise indoors and don't sweat profusely, which leaks yang

Recommended Recipes

Lamb and Ginger Soup

Ingredients: Lamb, ginger, scallion

Steps: Blanch the lamb, then simmer with ginger and scallion until tender.

Effect: Warms the kidneys, dispels cold, and consolidates essence.

Black Sesame Paste

Ingredients: Black sesame, glutinous rice flour, rock sugar

Steps: Dry-fry sesame until fragrant, grind to powder, cook into a paste, add rock sugar.

Effect: Tonifies the kidneys, blackens the hair, and stores essence.

Chinese Yam and Goji Soup

Ingredients: Chinese yam, goji berries, pork ribs

Steps: Cut the yam into chunks, simmer with goji berries and pork ribs.

Effect: Tonifies the kidneys, strengthens the spleen, and benefits essence.
