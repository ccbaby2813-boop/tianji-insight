Cold Dew Wellness

What is this about? Cold Dew (Oct 8-9) complete wellness guide: Metal element, Lungs organ. Three phenological phases: Wild geese arrive as guests, Sparrows enter the water and become clams, Chrysanthemums display yellow blooms. Cold Dew brings cool, dry weather, so moisten the lungs and stay warm. Add clothing morning and evening to avoid the chill—eat more pears, walnuts, and white fungus. Soak your feet before bed to warm the body and protect lung qi. Eat Pear, Sesame. Avoid Catching cold and getting the flu.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"Autumn qi connects with the lungs; the lungs dislike cold, and cold causes qi to contract."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · The Visceral Qi and the Seasons
Interpretation: Autumn qi corresponds to the lungs, and the lungs fear cold. During Cold Dew, as the weather turns cool, attention should be paid to keeping warm and guarding against cold, so as to protect and nourish the lungs.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'The lungs dislike cold.' By Cold Dew, even the dew has turned cold, so dress warmly and avoid getting chilled. Soak your feet before bed to warm the body and keep your lungs comfortably warm.

Action Items

  • Add clothing morning and night to prevent cold from harming the lungs
  • Soak feet before bed to warm the body and nourish yang qi
  • Eat more pears and walnuts to moisten the lungs and supplement qi

Recommended Recipes

Walnut and White Fungus Soup

Ingredients: Walnut · White fungus · Goji berries

Steps: Stew walnut kernels with rehydrated white fungus

Effect: Moistens the lungs, tonifies the kidneys, and sharpens the mind

Rock Sugar Snow Pear Bowl

Ingredients: Snow pear · Rock sugar · Lily bulb

Steps: Hollow out the pear, fill with lily bulb and rock sugar, then steam until tender

Effect: Moistens the lungs, prevents dryness, and stops cough

Sesame Honey Drink

Ingredients: Black sesame · Honey · Warm water

Steps: Dry-fry sesame until fragrant, grind to powder, and mix with honey in warm water

Effect: Resolves dryness, tonifies deficiency, and nourishes yin

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cold Dew?

Cold Dew is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around Oct 8-9. At this time: Wild geese arrive as guests, Sparrows enter the water and become clams, Chrysanthemums display yellow blooms. It marks the depth of autumn.

What is the wellness focus for Cold Dew?

The wellness focus for Cold Dew is Moisten the lungs, Keep warm, Prevent dryness. Cold Dew brings cool, dry weather, so moisten the lungs and stay warm. Add clothing morning and evening to avoid the chill—eat more Chinese yams, Chinese yams, and white fungus. Soak your feet before bed to warm the body and protect lung qi.

What should I eat during Cold Dew?

During Cold Dew, eat Pear, Sesame, Walnut, White fungus, Honey. Recommended recipes: Walnut and White Fungus Soup, Rock Sugar Snow Pear Bowl, Sesame Honey Drink.

What should I avoid during Cold Dew?

During Cold Dew, avoid: Catching cold and getting the flu, Spicy, hot, and drying foods, Excessive grief.
