Judgment & Image

Judgment (卦辞)
Favorable to persevere. Not eating at home brings good fortune. Favorable to cross the great river.
Image (象辞)
Heaven is stored within the mountain: Great Accumulation. Thus the superior person widely recalls the words and deeds of the past, to cultivate their virtue.

Personality Deep Dive

You are like a deep well: calm on the surface, full of water underneath. The Great Accumulation hexagram has Gen (Mountain) above and Qian (Heaven) below — heaven hidden inside the mountain. It's the image of the whole universe stored within a mountain, hence the name "Great Accumulation." The Judgement says: "Perseverance brings good fortune. It is auspicious to eat away from home." You are meant to wander and gather out in the world, not to stay cooped up at home. The Image says: "The superior person stores up many sayings and deeds of the past, to cultivate their virtue." Your core mode is "learning" — quietly packing knowledge into your mind, reading ten thousand books, traveling ten thousand miles, and turning it all into inner substance. You are not in a hurry; you know that anything truly powerful takes time. The price you pay is that sometimes you fill yourself so full you forget to pour it back out.

Emotional & Relationship Patterns

You are a slow-burn lover. The lower trigram Qian (Heaven) represents your inner strength and vitality; the upper trigram Gen (Mountain) represents your outer stillness. Inside you are opinionated and full of energy, but outside you appear composed and unhurried. In the Five Elements, Earth generates Metal (Qian is Metal): your inner energy is wrapped in outer calm, creating an emotional style that builds slowly and releases powerfully. You don't fall in love at first sight, but you grow to love over time. What you need is a partner who understands your silences — not someone who talks a lot, but someone who can read the meaning behind your quietness.

Strengths & Growth Blind Spots

Innate Talent
Your gift is 'building deep to release with force' — as the Image says, 'the superior person widely recalls the words and deeds of the past to cultivate virtue.' You have the ability to accumulate and distill. While others rush to express and prove themselves, you quietly gather. When the time is right, you strike with a breathtaking slam dunk.
Growth Blind Spot
Your blind spot is being too full — the Judgement says 'favorable to cross the great river,' urging you to act and move outward. You sometimes focus so much on accumulating that you forget to express. Another blind spot is being addicted to waiting — you always feel 'not ready yet,' but opportunity doesn't wait. Sometimes done is better than perfect.

Frequently Asked Questions about Hexagram 26 Dachu

Quick answers about Dachu personality, career, compatibility, and fortune

What is the personality of someone with Hexagram Dachu?

Hexagram Dachu represents the great accumulator of knowledge and wealth. People with this hexagram typically have distinctive traits and behavioral patterns, showing a unique style in relationships and career development. Understanding your hexagram personality helps you leverage strengths and address blind spots.

What careers suit Hexagram Dachu?

People with Hexagram Dachu often excel in academia, research, knowledge management, education. Choosing a career that matches your hexagram traits increases the likelihood of fulfillment and sustained growth. Of course, career choices should also consider personal interests, abilities, and market opportunities.

Which hexagram is most compatible with Dachu?

Hexagram Dachu pairs best with Wuwang (accumulation requires authenticity). The two hexagrams complement or resonate with each other in personality, making it easier to understand one another and build rapport in relationships and partnerships. Compatibility is a reference point; real relationships require mutual effort and commitment.

Is Hexagram Dachu auspicious or inauspicious for fortune-telling?

Hexagram Dachu: auspicious: great accumulation, wait for the right time. In the I Ching, auspiciousness is not absolute; it indicates the attitude and action appropriate for a given situation. Even an inauspicious hexagram can be navigated safely by upholding correctness and acting cautiously. Even an auspicious hexagram can lead to misfortune if one becomes complacent.
