Cui (Gathering Together)

Key takeaways: This article covers the core concepts, practical applications, and common questions about hexagram 45 cui (gathering together): personality, love & compatibility.

Judgment & Image

Judgment (卦辞)
Success. The king draws near to his ancestral temple. It is favorable to see the great person. Success and perseverance. Offering a great sacrifice brings good fortune, and it is favorable to undertake something.
Image (象辞)
The lake over the earth: the image of Gathering. The superior person thus renews weapons and guards against the unexpected.

Personality Deep Dive

Your core is a magnet—the lake waters gather upon the earth; water flows to low places, and people gather toward you. The Judgment says "the king arrives at his ancestral temple"—a king unites hearts through temple rites; you don't have to pull people; you naturally carry charisma. The Image says "the superior person removes weapons and guards against the unexpected"—after gathering people, you still need to keep order; you can not only get the group together but also command the room. Inner Kun (deep virtue, tolerance) = your heart is generous and can hold all kinds of people; Outer Dui (joy, sincerity) = you show genuine joy that makes people want to draw near. You are like Liu Bang (founder of the Han dynasty)—not the most capable personally, but able to bring Zhang Liang (strategist), Xiao He (chancellor), and Han Xin (military general) together, letting each play to their strengths. In your friends' eyes, you're the one who "organizes the group"—you arrange the gatherings, you lead the projects, you even created the WeChat group.

Emotional & Relationship Patterns

In relationships, you are the "gathering-type partner"—your circle of friends spans eight different circles, and in each one they consider you "one of their own." Inner Dui, Outer Kun—sincerity plus tolerance; you sustain every connection with warmth. Lake gathers on earth; in love you are the one always making arrangements—you know your partner's needs better than they do; when a friend is in trouble, you're already working on a solution before they even ask. Your love is warm and comfortable like the earth. But your issue is: you're kind to everyone, so it's easy for people to wonder whether you truly like them or are just habitually warm.

Strengths & Growth Blind Spots

Innate Talent
Your gift is "gathering people"—no need to sweet-talk or pump people up; after spending a short while with you, others think, "This person is reliable." The Judgment says "the king approaches his ancestral temple": you have natural charisma. This ability is a rare resource when teamwork is needed.
Growth Blind Spot
Your blind spot is "gathering without discernment"—you can gather people but can't tell who is sincere and who is false. The Image says "guard against the unforeseen," reminding you to stay on guard even as you draw others in. You pursue "unity" so much that you accept everyone for the sake of harmony, and sometimes you end up raising an ingrate. Learn to filter: not everyone deserves to be gathered.

Frequently Asked Questions about Hexagram 45 Cui

Quick answers about Cui personality, career, compatibility, and fortune

What is the personality of someone with Hexagram Cui?

Hexagram Cui represents the organizer bringing people together. People with this hexagram typically have distinctive traits and behavioral patterns, showing a unique style in relationships and career development. Understanding your hexagram personality helps you leverage strengths and address blind spots.

What careers suit Hexagram Cui?

People with Hexagram Cui often excel in community operations, event planning, HR, management. Choosing a career that matches your hexagram traits increases the likelihood of fulfillment and sustained growth. Of course, career choices should also consider personal interests, abilities, and market opportunities.

Which hexagram is most compatible with Cui?

Hexagram Cui pairs best with Sheng (gathering then rising). The two hexagrams complement or resonate with each other in personality, making it easier to understand one another and build rapport in relationships and partnerships. Compatibility is a reference point; real relationships require mutual effort and commitment.

Is Hexagram Cui auspicious or inauspicious for fortune-telling?

Hexagram Cui: auspicious: gathering and assembling, use ritual and ceremony. In the I Ching, auspiciousness is not absolute; it indicates the attitude and action appropriate for a given situation. Even an inauspicious hexagram can be navigated safely by upholding correctness and acting cautiously. Even an auspicious hexagram can lead to misfortune if one becomes complacent.
