Minor Heat Wellness

What is this about? Minor Heat (Jul 6-8) complete wellness guide: Fire element, Heart organ. Three phenological phases: Warm winds arrive, Crickets dwell in walls, Hawks begin to hunt. Minor Heat is muggy and hot; clear the heart and generate fluids. Eat less spicy and grilled food, don't stay up late, eat more mung beans, watermelon, and sour plum soup, and listen to soothing music to calm the mind. Eat Mung beans, Watermelon. Avoid Rage.

Key takeaways: This article covers the core concepts, practical applications, and common questions about minor heat wellness.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"Let your will be free of anger, let the flowering become elegant, let the qi be released, as if you loved being outdoors — this is the response to summer qi and the way to nurture growth."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · On Regulating the Spirit with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: In summer, keep your emotions calm, do not get angry, let your spirit be as radiant as blossoming plants, and allow the qi to flow outward freely.
Plain Language: The ancients said to 'let the qi be released.' In damp and hot weather, you especially need to open your heart and keep your qi flowing smoothly — don't bottle it up and stifle yourself.

Action Items

  • Drink mung bean and sour plum soup to clear the heart and relieve summer heat
  • Listen to soothing music to calm the mind
  • Take three deep breaths to release agitated qi

Recommended Recipes

Mung Bean and Mint Soup

Ingredients: Mung beans, mint, rock sugar

Steps: Boil the mung beans until tender; add mint just before serving

Effect: Clears heat, relieves summer heat, generates fluids

Sour Plum Soup

Ingredients: Smoked plums, hawthorn, rock sugar

Steps: Simmer smoked plums and hawthorn for 30 minutes, then add rock sugar

Effect: Generates fluids, quenches thirst, stimulates appetite

Cucumber Salad

Ingredients: Cucumber, garlic, vinegar

Steps: Smash the cucumber, toss with minced garlic and vinegar

Effect: Clears heat, drains dampness, stimulates appetite

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Minor Heat?

Minor Heat is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around Jul 6-8. At this time: Warm winds arrive, Crickets dwell in walls, Hawks begin to hunt. It marks the depth of summer.

What is the wellness focus for Minor Heat?

The wellness focus for Minor Heat is Clear the heart, Generate fluids, Dispel dampness. Minor Heat is muggy and hot; clear the heart and generate fluids. Eat less spicy and grilled food, don't stay up late, eat more luffas, luffa, and sour plum soup, and listen to soothing music to calm the mind.

What should I eat during Minor Heat?

During Minor Heat, eat Mung beans, Watermelon, Mint, Coix seed, Sour plum soup. Recommended recipes: Mung Bean and Mint Soup, Sour Plum Soup, Cucumber Salad.

What should I avoid during Minor Heat?

During Minor Heat, avoid: Rage, Spicy and grilled food, Staying up late, Excessive exercise.
