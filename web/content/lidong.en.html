Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The three months of winter are called closing and storing. Water freezes and the earth cracks open. Do not disturb yang. Go to bed early and rise late, always waiting for the sunlight."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Regulating the Spirit with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: Winter has begun; all things close and store. One should retire early and rise late, always waiting for the sunlight, and not disturb yang qi.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'Retire early and rise late, waiting for the sunlight.' In winter, don't get up too early and race against the sun. Follow the principle of closing and storing, and keep your yang qi well hidden.

Action Items

  • Sleep early, rise late, and wait for the sun before being active
  • Get plenty of sun during the day to nourish yang qi
  • Eat more mutton and walnuts to warm and tonify kidney yang
