Grain Rain Wellness

What is this about? Grain Rain (Apr 19-21) complete wellness guide: Earth element, Spleen organ. Three phenological phases: Duckweed begins to appear, Cuckoos ruffle their feathers, Hoopoes alight on mulberry trees. Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The spleen is (the organ of) Earth and governs the central region; it nourishes the four viscera through the four seasons, with its governance entrusted during the last eighteen days of each season."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen): Discourse on Taiyin and Yangming
Interpretation: Guyu is the time of late spring when Earth qi thrives and the spleen governs. One should strengthen the spleen and drain dampness to prepare for summer.
Plain Language: The ancients said that when the spleen and stomach are in command, during Guyu—the dampest time of late spring—they are most easily worn out. Strengthen the spleen and drain dampness so you don't let it soak in water.

Action Items

  • Eat more coix seed and Chinese yam congee to strengthen the spleen
  • Massage the abdomen after meals to aid digestion
  • Reduce idle overthinking to avoid worry harming the spleen

Recommended Recipes

Coix Seed and Winter Melon Soup

Ingredients: Coix seed · winter melon · ginger slices

Steps: Boil the coix seed first, then add winter melon slices and cook until tender.

Effect: Strengthens spleen, drains dampness, and clears heat

Tangerine Peel Duck Soup

Ingredients: Tangerine peel · duck meat · ginger

Steps: Blanch the duck, then simmer with tangerine peel for one hour.

Effect: Regulates qi, strengthens spleen, and drains dampness

White Lentil and Pork Rib Stew

Ingredients: White lentils · pork ribs · ginger

Steps: Soak the lentils until rehydrated, then stew with ribs until very tender.

Effect: Strengthens spleen, resolves dampness, nourishes stomach
