Grain in Ear Wellness

What is this about? Grain in Ear (Jun 5-7) complete wellness guide: Fire element, Heart organ. Three phenological phases: Praying mantises hatch, The shrike begins to call, The mockingbird falls silent. During Grain in Ear, the weather turns hot; focus on clearing the heart and relieving heat. Sweat sparingly and avoid spicy foods. Eat more watermelon, smoked plums, and winter melon. Take a midday nap, quiet your mind, and nourish your spirit. Eat Watermelon, Smoked plum. Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The heart governs summer, rules the blood and vessels, and stores the spirit."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · The Visceral Qi and the Seasons
Interpretation: The heart governs summer, controls the blood vessels, and stores the spirit. As the weather heats up during Grain in Ear, the focus should be on clearing the heart and nourishing the spirit.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'The heart rules the blood vessels and stores the spirit.' The heart is the body's master switch. In hot weather, you must nurture the heart. When the heart is calm, the body naturally feels cool—so don't be irritable.

Action Items

  • Take a midday nap to restore heart energy
  • Eat watermelon and smoked plum to relieve heat and generate fluids
  • Take an evening stroll to keep your heart calm and naturally cool

Recommended Recipes

Mung Bean Soup

Ingredients: Mung beans · rock sugar · water

Steps: Cook mung beans until they split open, then add rock sugar.

Effect: Clears the heart, relieves heat, and detoxifies.

Smoked Plum and Hawthorn Drink

Ingredients: Smoked plums · hawthorn · rock sugar

Steps: Boil the smoked plums and hawthorn, add rock sugar, and chill.

Effect: Generates fluids, quenches thirst, and stimulates the appetite.

Winter Melon and Coix Seed Soup

Ingredients: Winter melon · coix seeds · ginger

Steps: Simmer the coix seeds, then add sliced winter melon and cook together.

Effect: Clears heat, resolves dampness, and strengthens the spleen.
