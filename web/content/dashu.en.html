Major Heat Wellness

What is this about? Major Heat (Jul 22-24) complete wellness guide: Earth element, Spleen organ. Three phenological phases: Rotten grass turns into fireflies, The earth is moist and the summer air is sultry, Heavy rains come at the right time. During Great Heat, dampness and heat are at their peak. Strengthen the spleen and dispel dampness. Avoid cold drinks and greasy foods; eat more coix seed, winter melon, and lotus leaf. Escape the heat, take a midday rest, and calm the mind to nourish the spleen. Eat Coix seed and red bean porridge, Chinese yam. Avoid Greasy foods are hard to digest.

Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The spleen governs late summer, with the Foot Taiyin and Yangming channels in charge of its treatment. The spleen pertains to Earth and is at its height in late summer."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · The Law of the Viscera and the Seasons
Interpretation: Great Heat marks the end of summer when Earth qi reigns. As the spleen-earth is in command, the weather is oppressively hot and humid, so one should strengthen the spleen and dispel dampness.
Plain Language: The ancients said the spleen-earth is in charge during this time. Great Heat is damp and stuffy, and the spleen and stomach suffer most. You should strengthen the spleen and dispel dampness—don't let them get damp, tired, and then feed them ice.

Recommended Recipes

Coix Seed and Red Bean Porridge

Ingredients: Coix seed · red beans · rock sugar

Steps: Soak coix seed and red beans, then cook together into porridge.

Effect: Strengthens the spleen, dispels dampness, and promotes diuresis.

Winter Melon and Lotus Leaf Soup

Ingredients: Winter melon · lotus leaf · ginger

Steps: Cut winter melon into chunks and simmer with lotus leaf.

Effect: Clears heat, dispels dampness, and relieves summer heat.

Agastache Tofu Salad

Ingredients: Tofu · agastache leaves · light soy sauce

Steps: Cut tofu into pieces, mix with chopped agastache and light soy sauce.

Effect: Strengthens the spleen, resolves dampness, and awakens the spleen.
