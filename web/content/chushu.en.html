End of Heat Wellness

What is this about? End of Heat (Aug 22-24) complete wellness guide: Metal element, Lungs organ. Three phenological phases: Eagles offer birds in sacrifice, Heaven and Earth begin to turn solemn, Grain ripens. In Chushu, autumn dryness sets in; moisten the lungs and nourish yin. Huangdi Neijing Quote

"Keep the will calm and tranquil, to temper autumn's severity; gather and restrain the spirit and qi, so that the autumn air becomes level and even."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · On Regulating the Spirit in Accordance with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: Keep the spirit at peace to ease autumn's harsh and killing qi; collect and focus your vitality, so that the autumn qi is settled and calm.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'Gather your spirit and qi.' Chushu is the time when autumn deepens, so begin to draw your mind inward and rest quietly—don't let your heart still run wild as it did in summer.

Action Items

  • Sleep early and rise early to gather spirit and qi
  • Eat more pears and white fungus to moisten lungs and prevent dryness
  • Sit quietly to nourish the spirit; avoid excessive autumn sadness

Recommended Recipes

White Fungus and Pear Soup

Ingredients: White fungus · Snow pear · Rock sugar

Steps: Soak white fungus until soft, then simmer with snow pear for 40 minutes.

Effect: Moistens lungs and nourishes yin; prevents autumn dryness.

Sesame Glutinous Rice Porridge

Ingredients: Black sesame · Glutinous rice · Honey

Steps: Toast sesame until fragrant, grind to powder, then cook with glutinous rice into porridge.

Effect: Moistens dryness, nourishes yin, and calms the spirit.

Honey-Stewed Pear

Ingredients: Snow pear · Honey

Steps: Core the pear, add honey, and steam over water for 30 minutes.

Effect: Moistens lungs, promotes fluids, and relieves cough.

What is End of Heat?

End of Heat is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around Aug 22-24. At this time: Eagles offer birds in sacrifice, Heaven and Earth begin to turn solemn, Grain ripens. It marks the depth of autumn.

The wellness focus for End of Heat is Moisten lungs, Nourish yin, Calm spirit. In Chushu, autumn dryness sets in; moisten the lungs and nourish yin. Avoid cold foods and strenuous exercise. Eat more Chinese yams, Chinese yam, and white fungus. Go to bed early and rise early, keeping your mind calm and focused.
