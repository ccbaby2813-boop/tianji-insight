Frequently Asked Questions

How is Soul Match calculated?

Soul Match is based on Shao Yong's Plum Blossom Numerology from the Song Dynasty, combined with the personality traits of all 64 hexagrams. Each of the 2080 combinations includes a relationship name, resonance points, conflict points, and advice.

What are the match levels?

Match levels include: Heaven-Made Match, Soul Mate, Kindred Spirit, Complementary Destiny, Needs Work, Requires Adjustment, and more. Higher levels indicate greater compatibility in personality, values, and relationship dynamics.

How do I find my birth hexagram?

Download the Tianji Tuiyan app, enter your birth date and time. The app calculates your birth hexagram using Plum Blossom Numerology and provides a full personality reading, emotional patterns, strengths, blind spots, and historical figure parallels.

Are the compatibility results accurate?

Soul Match is a personality analysis tool based on I Ching hexagram relationships. It provides insights into personality tendencies and relationship patterns, and should not replace professional psychological counseling or relationship therapy.
