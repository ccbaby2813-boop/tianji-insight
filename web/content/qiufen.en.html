Autumn Equinox Wellness

What is this about? Autumn Equinox (Sep 22-24) complete wellness guide: Metal element, Lungs organ. Three phenological phases: Thunder begins to withdraw its voice, Hibernating insects seal their earthen doors, Waters begin to dry up. The Autumn Equinox is a time of balance; nourish the lungs and gather inward. Avoid great grief or great joy, eat less spicy and fried food, and enjoy more snow fungus and autumn pears. Huangdi Neijing Quote

"Do not let your will turn outward; keep the lung qi clear. This is answering the autumn qi and the way of fostering and gathering."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Regulating the Spirit with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: Do not let your spirit race outward; keep the lung qi clear and pure. This is how you resonate with autumn qi and walk the path of nurturing and gathering.
Plain Language: The ancients said, 'keep the lung qi clear.' The Autumn Equinox is the time when yin and yang are perfectly balanced. Gather your mind and spirit, let your lung qi be fresh and clear, and do not keep scattering your energy.

Action Items

Recommended Recipes

Snow Fungus and Autumn Pear Soup

Ingredients: Snow fungus, autumn pear, rock sugar

Steps: Soak the snow fungus, then simmer with the autumn pear

Effect: Moistens the lungs, promotes fluids, nourishes yin

Dried Persimmon and Snow Fungus Soup

Ingredients: Dried persimmons, snow fungus, longan

Steps: Slice dried persimmons and cook with snow fungus and longan

Effect: Moistens the lungs, stops cough, nourishes blood

Sesame Spinach Salad

Ingredients: Sesame seeds, spinach, garlic

Steps: Blanch the spinach, then toss with toasted sesame seeds and minced garlic

Effect: Moistens dryness, nourishes blood, strengthens yin

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Autumn Equinox?

Autumn Equinox is one of the 24 solar terms, usually around Sep 22-24. At this time: Thunder begins to withdraw its voice, Hibernating insects seal their earthen doors, Waters begin to dry up. It marks the depth of autumn.

What is the wellness focus for Autumn Equinox?

The wellness focus for Autumn Equinox is Nourish the lungs, Equilibrium, Gather inward. The Autumn Equinox is a time of balance; nourish the lungs and gather inward. Avoid great grief or great joy, eat less spicy and fried food, and enjoy more snow fungus and honeys. Calm your mind, collect your spirit, sleep early, and nurture this season of harvest.

What should I eat during Autumn Equinox?

During Autumn Equinox, eat Dried persimmons, Snow fungus, Lily bulbs, Autumn pears, Sesame seeds. Recommended recipes: Snow Fungus and Autumn Pear Soup, Dried Persimmon and Snow Fungus Soup, Sesame Spinach Salad.

What should I avoid during Autumn Equinox?

During Autumn Equinox, avoid: Great grief or great joy, Excessive dissipation of energy, Cold and coolness harming the lungs.
