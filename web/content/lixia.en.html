Huangdi Neijing Quote

"The three months of summer are called 'the flourishing of growth.' Heaven and earth breath together, and all things put forth flowers and bear fruit. Go to bed late and rise early, and have no aversion to the long days."
— Plain Questions (Su Wen) · Regulating the Spirit with the Four Seasons
Interpretation: Summer begins, all things flourish. One should go to bed late and rise early, not dislike the long days, and keep the spirit free of anger.
Plain Language: The ancients said 'do not tire of the sun.' In summer the days are long; don't let the heat irritate you. Stay calm and free of anger, so your body's qi flows smoothly.
